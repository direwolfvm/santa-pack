# How Santa Pack works

Santa Pack is a family gift exchange with a guessing game built in. Everyone gives presents in secret, each present gets a mysterious "cloaked" name, and when the presents are opened, each person guesses who gave theirs. Then the giver is revealed.

This guide is the reference for the whole game: who can do what, what each stage looks like, and who can see what. It is also the Help page inside the app.

## The game in one minute

1. A family **manager** starts a **gift round**, for example "Christmas Eve 2026".
2. **Giving:** everyone adds the presents they're giving. Each present gets a cloaked name such as "Operation Mistletoe", and optionally photos of the gift and of it wrapped.
3. **Guessing:** the manager starts guessing. Each person sees their own presents only by cloaked name and wrapped photo. They open the present, can snap a photo of what's inside, and guess who gave it.
4. **Unwrap it:** when they're ready, each person reveals the giver of their present and finds out if they guessed right.
5. **The reveal:** the manager reveals everything, and the whole family sees who gave what, every photo, and who guessed best.

## Who's who

| Role                          | What they can do                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Player**                    | Anyone in a family. Gives presents, guesses, and unwraps their own.                                                                                                                             |
| **Manager**                   | A player who also runs the family: lets people in, starts rounds, moves a round from stage to stage, and plays for family members without an account. Managers can't peek at anyone's presents. |
| **Player without an account** | Someone who won't sign in, such as a small child or a grandparent. A manager adds them and gives, guesses and unwraps for them.                                                                 |
| **Site admin**                | Looks after the whole site: creates families, adds people to them, and can see every present.                                                                                                   |

A family can have more than one manager. Managers belong to one family; being a manager in one family gives no rights in another.

## Getting started

### Make an account

1. Go to santapack.me and fill in **Create an account** with your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters.
2. Santa Pack emails you a link from `noreply@mg.santapack.me`. Open it to confirm your email address. If it hasn't arrived after a few minutes, check your spam folder or use **Send the email again**.
3. Back in Santa Pack, choose **I've confirmed my email**.

Forgot your password? On the sign-in page, open **Forgot your password?** and enter your email. The link in the email works once and expires after an hour. You can also send yourself a reset link from **your profile** (your name, top right).

### Join a family

There are two ways in:

- **With a join code.** Ask a manager for the family's 8-character join code (it's on their family page). Enter it under **Join a family** on your home page. You'll wait in the queue until a manager lets you in.
- **Added by a site admin**, who can put you straight into a family.

You can leave a family at any time with **Leave this family** at the bottom of its page.

## A gift round, step by step

Every round moves through three stages, shown by the badge at the top of the round page: **giving**, **guessing** and **complete**.

### 1. Giving

Open the round from your family page and use **Add a gift**:

- **Who is it for?** Anyone else in the family.
- **The gift.** What it really is. Only you can see this until it's unwrapped.
- **Cloaked name.** A mysterious name the receiver sees while guessing, such as "Operation Mistletoe". Make it fun, not a giveaway.
- **Notes.** Anything else, for example where you hid it. Also private until it's unwrapped.

Choose **Wrap it up**. The present appears under **Your gifts**, where you can **Edit** or **Remove** it, and add photos:

- **The gift:** a photo of the present itself, before you wrap it. It stays secret until the receiver unwraps it in the app.
- **Wrapped:** a photo of it wrapped. The receiver sees this while guessing, so they know which package is which.

Both photos are optional. You can change gifts and photos as long as the round is in giving.

While giving, nobody else can see your presents, not even the receiver or a manager. The round progress shows only how many gifts each person has wrapped, so managers can tell who still has shopping to do.

### 2. Guessing

When everyone is ready, a manager chooses **Start guessing**. Gifts can't be changed after that, unless a manager moves the round **Back to giving**.

Under **Gifts for you**, each present shows its cloaked name and the giver's wrapped photo, if they took one. For each one:

1. **Open the present** (the real one).
2. **Take a photo** of it unwrapped, if you like, under **Unwrapped**. This photo is yours. The giver can see it too, and the whole family will at the reveal.
3. **Guess who gave it** with **Who gave this?** and **Save guess**. You can change your guess as often as you like until you unwrap it in the app.
4. **Unwrap it.** This locks your guess and reveals the gift's name, notes, the giver, and the giver's photo of the gift, next to your own.

If you choose **Unwrap it** without a guess, you can't guess that one afterwards.

Under **Gifts you gave**, givers can follow along: who their presents are for, and the receiver's guess once they've unwrapped it.

While guessing, the round progress shows only totals ("4 gifts, 2 guessed, 1 unwrapped"), because counts per person could give away who gave to whom.

### 3. The reveal

When the guessing is done, a manager chooses **Reveal everything**. Everyone in the family now sees every present: who gave it, who got it, the guess, whether it was right, and all the photos. **Best guessers** shows who knew their Santas best.

A manager can **Reopen guessing** if the reveal came too early.

## Photos

Each present can have up to three photos. All of them are optional.

| Photo         | Taken by     | When     | Who sees it before the reveal                                                 |
| ------------- | ------------ | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The gift**  | The giver    | Giving   | Only the giver. The receiver sees it once they unwrap the present in the app. |
| **Wrapped**   | The giver    | Giving   | The giver, and the receiver during guessing                                   |
| **Unwrapped** | The receiver | Guessing | The receiver and the giver                                                    |

When the round is complete, the whole family sees every photo.

**On a phone,** each photo has two buttons. **Take photo** opens the camera, and **Choose photo** lets you pick one from your photo library (or take one). On a computer, **Add photo** lets you pick a file. There's nothing to install: this all works in the phone's web browser.

Photos are made smaller before they're uploaded, which also removes hidden details such as where the photo was taken. They're stored privately and are only ever shown to the people allowed to see them.

## Who can see what

|                                                    | Giving                                 | Guessing                                                                                                   | Complete   |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| **The giver**                                      | Everything about their own gifts       | Everything about their own gifts, plus the receiver's unwrapped photo, and their guess once they unwrap it | Everything |
| **The receiver**                                   | Nothing                                | Cloaked name, wrapped photo, their own guess and photo. Everything else once they choose **Unwrap it**.    | Everything |
| **Everyone else in the family, managers included** | How many gifts each person has wrapped | Totals only                                                                                                | Everything |
| **Site admins**                                    | Everything                             | Everything                                                                                                 | Everything |

These rules are enforced by Santa Pack's server, not just hidden on the screen, so nothing secret reaches anyone's phone or computer before it should.

## For managers

Your family page has an **Elf desk** with your tools:

- **Join code.** Share it with your family. **New join code** makes a fresh one if the old one got around; the old one stops working.
- **Waiting to join.** **Let them in** or **Decline** people who used the join code.
- **Add someone without an account.** Enter their name and **Add player**.
- **Start a new round.** Name it and choose **Start round**.

In the **Who's playing** list you can make someone a manager, make a manager a member again, or remove someone.

On a round page, **Round controls** move the round along: **Start guessing**, **Reveal everything**, and the backwards steps **Back to giving** and **Reopen guessing**. Moving a round never shows you anyone's presents.

### Playing for someone without an account

On a round page, use **Playing as** to switch to a family member without an account. You then see exactly what they would see, and can give, guess, take their photos and unwrap for them. Switch back to **Myself** to play as yourself. You can only play for people without an account, never for someone who has one.

## For site admins

The **Admin** page (in the menu) lists every account and family:

- Create a family, and add people to it directly as a member or manager.
- Make someone a site admin or remove that, and turn accounts off or back on.

Site admins can see every present, so give this role only to people you'd trust with the secrets. Deleting a family or a round also deletes its presents and their photos.

## Using Santa Pack on your phone

Santa Pack is a website, so there's no app to install. To make it feel like one, add it to your home screen:

- **iPhone or iPad (Safari):** tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

The camera works from the website. The first time you choose **Take photo**, your phone may ask whether Santa Pack can use the camera.

## Questions

**I didn't get the confirmation or reset email.** Check spam, and look for mail from `noreply@mg.santapack.me`. You can send a confirmation email again from the "Check your inbox" page, and a reset link from the sign-in page. Requests are limited to a few an hour.

**I made a mistake in my gift.** Edit it while the round is in giving. If guessing has started, ask a manager to move the round **Back to giving**, fix it, and have them start guessing again.

**I unwrapped a present by mistake.** Unwrapping in the app can't be undone, and the guess stays as it was.

**Can I change my unwrapped photo after unwrapping?** Yes, as long as the round is still in guessing.

**Someone in the family doesn't want an account.** A manager can add them with **Add someone without an account** and play for them.

**Can managers see my presents?** No. Managers run the round but see presents only when the round is complete, like everyone else. Only site admins can see everything.
